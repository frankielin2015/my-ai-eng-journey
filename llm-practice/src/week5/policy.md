# Acme Retail Customer Policy

This document describes how Acme Retail handles refunds, returns, shipping,
and delivery disputes. It is the canonical reference for support agents and for
the retrieval-augmented assistant that answers customer questions. If a
customer's situation is not covered here, escalate to a human supervisor.

## Refund Window

Customers may request a full refund within 30 calendar days of the delivery
date shown by the carrier. The refund window runs from delivery, not from the
order date, because customers cannot evaluate an item they have not received.
For orders marked lost in transit, the window starts from the original
estimated delivery date. Refunds requested between day 31 and day 60 are issued
as store credit at a supervisor's discretion. After day 60, refunds are not
available except where required by law or where the item is defective under the
manufacturer's warranty.

## Refund Method and Timing

Approved refunds return to the original payment method. Credit card refunds
appear within five to ten business days depending on the issuing bank, while
debit and digital wallet refunds usually settle within three business days. If
the original payment method has expired or been closed, we issue store credit
instead. Shipping charges are refunded only when the return results from our
error, such as a wrong item or a damaged shipment.

## Returning an Item

To start a return, the customer opens the order in their account and selects
"Return or replace". We generate a prepaid label unless the item is final sale.
Items must be returned in original packaging with all accessories, and
electronics must be factory reset before shipping. We inspect every return at
the warehouse; items damaged through customer mishandling may receive a partial
refund. Final sale items, personalized goods, and perishables cannot be
returned unless they arrived defective.

## Shipping Options and Costs

Standard shipping is free on orders over fifty dollars and otherwise costs five
dollars. Standard delivery takes three to five business days. Expedited
shipping costs fifteen dollars and arrives within two business days. Overnight
shipping costs twenty-nine dollars and must be ordered before two o'clock in
the afternoon local time to ship the same day. Alaska, Hawaii, and
international destinations are served by a partner carrier at higher rates.

## Delivery Disputes

If tracking shows a package as delivered but the customer cannot find it, the
customer should check porches, mailrooms, neighbors, and the front desk, then
wait twenty-four hours because carriers sometimes scan early. If it is still
missing, the customer must file a missing package claim within seven days of
the marked delivery date. We open a carrier investigation, which takes up to
ten business days. If the carrier cannot confirm delivery, we reship at no cost
or issue a full refund at the customer's choice.

## Lost and Delayed Shipments

When tracking has not updated for seven consecutive days, a shipment is stalled.
Agents may reship stalled domestic orders immediately without waiting for the
carrier investigation to close. When a shipment is confirmed lost, the customer
chooses either a replacement or a refund. We do not charge restocking or
reshipping fees for lost orders. Repeated claims on one account are reviewed by
the fraud team before a second reshipment is approved.

## Damaged or Incorrect Items

Customers must report damaged or incorrect items within fourteen days of
delivery and include photographs of the item and packaging. We cover return
shipping for these cases and ship the replacement before receiving the original
when the account is in good standing. If the replacement is out of stock, we
issue a full refund including original shipping. Claims filed after fourteen
days are handled case by case and may require the item to be returned first.

## Subscription and Billing Disputes

Duplicate subscription charges are refunded in full once verified. Customers
may cancel a subscription at any time, and cancellation stops the next renewal
but does not refund the current period unless the charge was made in error. If
a customer was charged after a cancellation they can prove, we refund the
charge and file an internal ticket to investigate the billing bug. Billing
disputes must be raised within 90 days of the charge.
